Frequently Asked Questions (Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric)

How many showers can the Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric run at the same time?

Standard modern showerheads use 2.0 GPM. In warm southern climates (50°F temperature rise), the Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric delivers 2.4 GPM—enough for 1 simultaneous showers plus a faucet. In freezing northern winters (75°F temperature rise), output is 1.6 GPM (1 simultaneous showers).

How do you calculate GPM for the Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric at any groundwater temperature?

Use the thermodynamic heat-transfer formula: GPM = (60,804 Effective BTU/hr) ÷ (500 × ΔT), where ΔT is Target Hot Water Temp (120°F) minus Incoming Groundwater Temp.

What gas line or electrical breaker is required for the Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric?

The Rheem RTEX-18 (18kW 240V) Tankless Electric requires 2x 40A Double-Pole Breakers (2x 8 AWG) along with a dedicated 3/4-inch cold water supply and 150 PSI / 200,000 BTU ASME temperature-and-pressure (T&P) relief valve.

Frequently Asked Questions (Code 12)

How many showers can the Code 12 run at once?

With a 199,000 BTU/hr burner operating at 96% efficiency, the unit provides 7.6 GPM at a 50°F rise (supporting up to 3 to 4 simultaneous standard 2.0 GPM showers in warm climates/summer) and 5.1 GPM at a 75°F winter rise (supporting 2 to 3 simultaneous showers comfortably in freezing climates).

How does groundwater temperature affect the Code 12?

Flow rate is governed by the thermodynamic formula GPM = (199,000 BTU/hr × 0.96) / (ΔT × 500). When incoming groundwater is 70°F (50°F rise to 120°F), the unit delivers 7.6 GPM, but when winter ground water drops to 45°F (75°F rise), available flow rate reduces to 5.1 GPM.

What gas line or breaker size is needed for the Code 12?

The unit requires a 3/4-inch dedicated gas supply line sized for 199,000 BTU/hr with dynamic inlet gas pressures typically between 3.5" to 10.5" W.C. for Natural Gas (8.0" to 13.0" W.C. for LP), dual-pipe 2-inch PVC/CPVC/polypropylene venting, and a standard 120V/15A dedicated electrical outlet for internal controls.
